## New member intake
Karate is a versatile form of training that offers personal growth, a social environment, and a better foundation for succeeding with your studies in a hectic everyday life. Universitetets karateklubb (UKK) is the oldest karate club on the Hill. We train the style Chito-ryu, but as a student club we're also open to members who have trained other styles.

Karate is a form of training that suits everyone. You don't need any prior knowledge or skills - you'll pick those up along the way.

We accept new beginners all year round, but we also cooperate with Sammen treningssenter on a free trial period in August (weeks 33-34) and January (weeks 3-4). Your first sessions with us are always free, but you'll otherwise need to pay for entry to our dojo yourself.

Feel free to contact us for more information (email: styret.ukk@gmail.com) or just show up at one of our beginners' sessions in light training clothes.

**Training times** can be found [here](/en/trening/treningstider).

## Membership in Universitetets karateklubb

Universitetets karateklubb is affiliated with the Norwegian Martial Arts Federation/Norwegian Olympic and Paralympic Committee and Confederation of Sports (NIF).

To **become a member**, register via this [membership link](https://medlemskap.nif.no/24963); if you run into problems during registration, just get in touch via styret.ukk@gmail.com.

The membership fee for the 2024-2025 training year is NOK 300. In addition, you'll need to pay a training fee of NOK 400/semester. To get access to [Sammen City](https://sammen.no/no/trening/senter/city-treningssenter) and other training centres for students, you should also be a member of Sammen.
