### Other

We also hold regular weekend training sessions and take part in competitions nationally and internationally for those who are interested.

### Beginner intake

If you're new and want to try a training session with us, just show up barefoot and in light training clothes whenever it suits you.

At the start of each semester *(Spring) weeks 3-4* and *(Autumn) weeks 33-34*, we have an agreed trial period with Sammen treningssenter offering free entry to our dojo. For the rest of the year, the trial period with us is still free for the first few weeks, but you'll need to pay for entry to the training centre yourself.

Feel free to reach out beforehand or if you have any questions at all!
